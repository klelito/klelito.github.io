## Context

A manufacturer of TV lifts and rack cooling units used transformer power supplies in most of its TV lifts.

## Problem

The transformer design raised the production cost of the power supply and caused a large share of failures.

## Solution

I independently developed the switch to switch-mode power supplies: selected the solution, ran the tests and prepared the change for production under the lead electronics engineer's supervision.

## Outcome

Power-supply production cost fell by about 60% and its failure rate by about 40%.

## My role

- selecting the solution and designing the change,
- testing and verification,
- preparing the production rollout.

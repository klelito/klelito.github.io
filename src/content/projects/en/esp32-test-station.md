## Context

The R&D department of a TV-lift manufacturer needed a station for control and measurement while testing devices.

## Solution

The station combines C/C++ firmware on an ESP32 with a Python app with a Tkinter interface that drives the test, collects measurements and helps analyse them.

## My role

- ESP32 firmware,
- the Python control and data-analysis app,
- bringing the station into use in the R&D department.

## Context

A beekeeper wants to know what is happening in the hive without opening it.

## Solution

I fitted the hive with a scale, a camera and a set of sensors. The data is sent to a server, processed and presented to the user on a website and in a mobile app.

## My role

- electronics and sensors,
- data transmission and processing,
- visualisation on the website and in the app.

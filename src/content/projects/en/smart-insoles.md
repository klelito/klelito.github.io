## Context

A tech startup was developing an electronic shoe-insole system.

## Solution

I programmed AVR and ESP microcontrollers in C/C++ (bare-metal and PlatformIO), designed PCBs and selected components, brought up and soldered the boards, measured them and debugged faults. I wrote Python scripts that automate work on the production line.

## Outcome

The product's electronics and firmware, plus automation of selected production-line tasks.

## My role

- AVR and ESP firmware,
- PCB design, bring-up and debugging,
- Python scripts for production, 3D printing and electronics testing.

## Context

Transfers between warehouses were recorded on paper and later retyped into the ERP system.

## Problem

Retyping documents delayed stock levels and caused mistakes.

## Solution

A barcode-scanner terminal app that creates the transfer document directly in the ERP.

## Outcome

No transfer document is retyped by hand anymore, and stock levels are current immediately.

## My role

- designed the workflow together with the warehouse team,
- implemented the app and the ERP integration,
- trained the users.

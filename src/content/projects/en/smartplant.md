## Context

A personal project entered into the Polish national olympiad of technical innovation and invention.

## Solution

Based on humidity and temperature sensor data, the system controls watering, lighting, temperature and air circulation — pumps, fans, lighting and 230 V heating.

## Outcome

A working prototype and the finalist title in the Electronics and Automation category.

- electronics and microcontroller firmware design,

## Solution

I built a freestyle FPV drone with an analogue camera and frame parts made with 3D printing. The video is transmitted in real time to the pilot's goggles.

## Outcome

1st place at the school science fair.

## My role

- selecting and assembling the electronics,
- designing and printing the frame parts,
- setting up video transmission.
